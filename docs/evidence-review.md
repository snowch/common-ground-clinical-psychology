# Evidence review

The course uses established introductory constructs, selected scholarly work and linked professional guidance. Examples, figures and invented teaching numbers are original illustrations, not data from the cited papers.

Sources were checked for identity and availability. Selected professional recommendations and scholarly overviews were read against the scope of claims. This is a selective internal review, not a systematic literature review, independent academic peer review or clinical endorsement.

Core cautions remain in the visible evidence pages. Optional research panels provide sources without requiring them to follow the main lesson.

## Clinical scope

NICE recommendations were checked for shared decision making, assessment, benefits and harms, preferences, and avoiding psychologically focused debriefing for PTSD. Local professional practice and care routes can differ. The course offers no diagnostic checklist, personal score, treatment plan or symptom-collection flow.
